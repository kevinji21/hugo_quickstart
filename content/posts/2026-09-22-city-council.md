## Summary

The Palo Alto City Council held a special meeting on Tuesday, September 22, 2026, which included a closed session for sensitive city business and a public session focused on filling important community roles. The Council discussed real estate negotiations concerning the Cubberley Site and evaluated the City Manager's performance in private, before publicly interviewing candidates for vacancies on two key city commissions.

Key actions and discussions included:

*   Discussions in closed session regarding the potential purchase, exchange, or lease of the Cubberley Site with the Palo Alto Unified School District.
*   A private performance evaluation of the City Manager.
*   Public interviews with candidates applying for open positions on the Parks & Recreation Commission and the Public Art Commission.

## Agenda Highlights

**CLOSED SESSION**

*   **AA1. CONFERENCE WITH REAL PROPERTY NEGOTIATORS (Cubberley Site):** The Council met privately with city negotiators to discuss the terms for potentially buying, exchanging, or leasing the property at 4000 Middlefield Road, commonly known as the Cubberley Site. These negotiations are with the Palo Alto Unified School District.
*   **1. Public Employee Performance Evaluations (City Manager):** The Council held a private discussion to evaluate the performance of the City Manager.

**SPECIAL ORDERS OF THE DAY**

*   **2. Interview Candidates for Vacancies on the Parks & Recreation Commission (PRC) and Public Art Commission (PAC):** The Council publicly interviewed individuals who have applied to fill open seats on two important city commissions: the Parks & Recreation Commission, which advises on parks, open spaces, and recreational programs, and the Public Art Commission, which guides the city's public art initiatives.
