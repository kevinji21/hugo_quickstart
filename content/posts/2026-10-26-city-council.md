## Summary
Palo Alto City Council held a Special Meeting on Monday, October 26, 2026, addressing a range of important city matters. Key discussions and decisions included appointing members to city commissions, reviewing winter storm preparedness, approving several items on the consent calendar, and adopting new ordinances related to stream protection and a temporary ban on new detention facilities.

Key actions and discussion items included:
*   Appointed new members to the Public Art Commission and the Stormwater Management Oversight Committee.
*   Reviewed the city's plans for winter storm preparedness for 2026-2027.
*   Approved the minutes from the October 13, 2026 meeting.
*   Expanded funding for human services programs by an additional $428,000 annually.
*   Clarified zoning rules to allow multi-family housing with a special permit in certain commercial areas.
*   Updated the city's fee schedule for fiscal year 2027, including solar permit costs.
*   Adopted an ordinance to strengthen protections for stream corridors.
*   Imposed a temporary ban on new or expanded detention and custodial facilities within city limits.

## Agenda Highlights

*   **Appointments to City Commissions:** The Council appointed new members to serve on the Public Art Commission (PAC) and the Stormwater Management Oversight Committee (SWMOC).
*   **Closed Session - Property Negotiations:** Council members met privately to discuss potential negotiations regarding the price and payment terms for a property located at 1900 Embarcadero Road. No public action or decision was made during this session.
*   **Review of Winter Storm Preparedness:** The Council reviewed the city's activities and plans for preparing for the upcoming 2026-2027 winter storm season. This included discussions on the seasonal precipitation outlook, locations for sandbag stations, and how information will be shared with residents.
*   **Approval of Meeting Minutes:** The minutes from the City Council meeting held on October 13, 2026, were officially approved.
*   **Human Services Program Expansion:** The Council approved an expansion of the Human Services Resource Allocation Process (HSRAP) program, allocating an additional $428,000 in annual funding for fiscal years 2028-2029 to support vital community services.
*   **Zoning Clarification for Multi-Family Housing:** An ordinance was adopted to clarify the city's zoning rules. This change specifies that multi-family residential buildings can be allowed in certain commercial zones (specifically ROLM(E) districts) if they obtain a special use permit.
*   **Update to Municipal Fee Schedule:** An ordinance was adopted to update the city's schedule of fees for fiscal year 2027. This update also included findings about the reasonable cost for issuing permits for solar energy systems.
*   **Stream Corridor Protection Ordinance:** The Council adopted an ordinance to amend the Palo Alto Municipal Code, updating and strengthening the rules for protecting stream corridors within the city.
*   **Temporary Moratorium on Detention Facilities:** A temporary ordinance was adopted to place a ban, or moratorium, on the establishment of any new or expanded detention and custodial facilities within Palo Alto city limits.
*   **Information Reports:** The Council received two reports for their information: the Monthly Investment Report for September 2026 and the City's Energy Risk Management Report for the second half of Fiscal Year 2026. These reports were provided for review and did not require discussion or action during the meeting.
