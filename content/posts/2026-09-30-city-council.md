## Summary

The Palo Alto City Council held a Special Meeting on September 30, 2026, focusing on interviewing candidates for city commissions and conducting several confidential closed-session discussions. Key topics included filling vacancies on the Human Relations and Planning and Transportation Commissions, evaluating the performance of city leadership, discussing labor negotiations, and engaging in real property negotiations concerning the Cubberley Site.

Key actions and discussions included:
*   Interviewing candidates for open positions on the Human Relations Commission (HRC) and Planning and Transportation Commission (PTC).
*   Conducting performance evaluations for the City Clerk and City Attorney in a closed session.
*   Discussing labor negotiations with unrepresented employees, specifically the City Attorney, City Clerk, and City Manager, during a closed session.
*   Engaging in confidential negotiations with the Palo Alto Unified School District regarding the potential purchase, exchange, or lease of the Cubberley Site.

## Agenda Highlights

**SPECIAL ORDERS OF THE DAY**

*   **Item 3: Interview Candidates for Vacancies on the Human Relations Commission (HRC) and Planning and Transportation Commission (PTC)**
    The City Council interviewed individuals interested in serving on two important city commissions. The Human Relations Commission works on community relations and social equity issues, while the Planning and Transportation Commission advises the Council on land use, development projects, and transportation policies. These interviews are a crucial step in selecting community members to help guide city decisions.

**CLOSED SESSION**

*   **Item 1: Public Employee Performance Evaluations (City Clerk and City Attorney)**
    The Council met privately to discuss the job performance of two key city officials: the City Clerk and the City Attorney. These evaluations are confidential to ensure a fair and thorough review process.

*   **Item 2: Conference with Labor Negotiator (Unrepresented Employees: City Attorney, City Clerk and City Manager)**
    Council members held a confidential discussion with a labor negotiator regarding employment terms for the City Attorney, City Clerk, and City Manager. This session focused on matters related to these unrepresented employees' contracts or compensation.

*   **Item AA1: Conference with Real Property Negotiators (4000 Middlefield Road, known as the Cubberley Site)**
    The Council met in a closed session to discuss ongoing negotiations with the Palo Alto Unified School District concerning the Cubberley Site. The discussion focused on potential agreements for the property, including its purchase, exchange, or lease, and the financial terms involved. These negotiations are kept confidential to protect the city's bargaining position.
