## Summary
The Palo Alto City Council held a regular meeting on November 16, 2026, addressing a range of important topics for the community. Key discussions included the city's long-term water supply reliability and significant steps to address housing needs, including zoning changes for a specific property and advancing new affordable housing projects. The Council also approved several routine administrative items and funding agreements for infrastructure.

Key actions and discussions included:
*   A joint study session on Palo Alto's water-supply reliability.
*   Advancing a program to incentivize home building over commercial development.
*   Approving a funding agreement for the Newell Road/San Francisquito Creek Bridge replacement.
*   Authorizing the release of a Request for Proposals for the 340 Portage Avenue affordable housing development.
*   Final approval of updated permit fees for solar energy systems and other planning services.
*   Changing the zoning and land use designation for 470 Olive Avenue to allow for medium-density mixed-use development, supporting housing goals.
*   Approving amendments to the employment agreements for the City Manager, City Attorney, and City Clerk.

## Agenda Highlights

**STUDY SESSION**
1.  **Water-Supply Reliability:** The City Council and Utilities Advisory Commission held a joint discussion to review Palo Alto's water supply. This session included presentations from city staff and regional water agencies, focusing on ensuring the city has a reliable water source for the future. No decisions were made, as this was a discussion and information-gathering session.

**CONSENT CALENDAR**
The following items were approved as part of the Consent Calendar:
2.  **Approval of Minutes:** The minutes from the November 2, 2026, City Council meeting were approved.
3.  **Housing Element Program 3.9(A) Implementation:** The Council approved the implementation of a program designed to identify incentives that encourage the construction of homes instead of commercial buildings in specific strategic locations.
4.  **Newell Road/San Francisquito Creek Bridge Funding:** An agreement with the Santa Clara Valley Water District was approved, providing $1,555,050 in local matching funds for the construction of the Newell Road/San Francisquito Creek Bridge Replacement Project.
5.  **Conflict of Interest Code Amendment:** A resolution was adopted to update the city's Conflict of Interest Code for designated officers and employees, aligning it with state regulations.
6.  **Foothills Nature Preserve Improvements Ordinance:** An ordinance was adopted to allow for improvements at the Foothills Nature Preserve, as recommended by the Parks and Recreation Commission.
7.  **Below Market Rate Housing Program Audit:** The audit report for the Below Market Rate Housing Program and a follow-up report on Fiscal Year 2026 audit recommendations were approved.
8.  **340 Portage Avenue Affordable Housing Development:** The Council authorized the release of a Request for Proposals (RFP) and a local funding contribution for the development of affordable housing at 340 Portage Avenue (Matadero Creek site).
9.  **Solar Energy Systems Permit Fees (Second Reading):** The Council gave final approval to an ordinance that updates the fees for issuing permits for solar energy systems and other services provided by the Planning and Development Services Department for Fiscal Year 2027.

**ACTION ITEMS**
10. **470 Olive Avenue Zoning Change:** The City Council adopted an ordinance to change the zoning for 470 Olive Avenue from single-family residential to medium-density mixed-use. This change, along with an updated land use designation, aims to align the property with an adjacent parcel and add it to the city's housing inventory to meet housing requirements.
11. **Council Appointed Officers Employment Agreements:** Amendments to the employment agreements for the City Manager, City Attorney, and City Clerk were approved.
