## Summary
The Palo Alto City Council held a regular meeting on August 17, 2026, addressing a range of city business from housing policy to infrastructure and community services. Key discussions included updates to local Accessory Dwelling Unit rules, a significant housing development proposal under state "Builders Remedy" provisions, and several operational approvals.

*   **Accessory Dwelling Unit (ADU) Ordinance Adopted:** The Council approved changes to city rules for ADUs and Junior ADUs, aligning them with state laws and previous Council direction.
*   **762 San Antonio Road Project Considered:** A public hearing was held to review a proposal for an eight-story, 228-unit residential building, presented under the state's "Builders Remedy" law.
*   **City Electrification Efforts Advanced:** A contract was approved for design services to electrify city facilities.
*   **Overnight Warming/Cooling Program Expanded:** The program providing shelter during extreme weather was expanded, and its operating framework approved.
*   **Electric Supply Agreements Authorized:** The City Manager was authorized to manage electric supply transactions.

## Agenda Highlights

**Closed Session**
The Council met in closed session to discuss existing litigation involving Swinerton v. City of Palo Alto and to negotiate real property terms for the Cubberley Site (4000 Middlefield Road) with the Palo Alto Unified School District.

**In-Person Public Comment**
Residents had the opportunity to speak on topics not specifically listed on the agenda.

**Council Member Questions, Comments, Announcements**
Council members shared updates and made announcements.

**Consent Calendar**
Items on the Consent Calendar were approved in a single vote, as is typical for routine matters.
*   **3. Electric Supplies:** The Council authorized the City Manager to enter into agreements for the city's electricity needs.
*   **4. Overnight Warming/Cooling Location Program:** The Council approved expanding the city's program that provides shelter during extreme weather, along with its operational guidelines.
*   **5. Electrification of City Facilities:** A contract was approved with Salas O’Brien Engineers, Inc. for nearly $300,000 to design the electrification of various city buildings.

**City Manager Comments**
The City Manager provided updates to the Council and the public.

**Action Items**
*   **6. Accessory and Junior Accessory Dwelling Units (ADUs):** The Council adopted an ordinance to update various sections of the Palo Alto Municipal Code. These changes bring local ADU and Junior ADU rules in line with recent state laws and previous guidance from the City Council.
*   **7. PUBLIC HEARING: 762 San Antonio Road Residential Project:** The Council held a public hearing to consider a major architectural review application. The proposal involves demolishing three commercial buildings to construct an eight-story building with 228 rental apartments. This project was presented under the state's "Builders Remedy" provision, which allows developers to move forward with projects if a city has not met its state-mandated housing goals. The Council reviewed the plans and heard public and applicant presentations before making a decision.

**Virtual Public Comment**
Remote participants had the opportunity to speak on topics not on the agenda.

**Information Reports**
These reports were provided for the Council's and public's information and were not discussed during the meeting.
*   **A. 2100 Geng Road:** Report on the determination of impact fees following a fee protest.
*   **B. Boards, Commissions, and Committees:** A mid-year attendance report for 2026 was provided.
*   **C. Investment Activity Report:** The report detailed the city's investment activities for the fourth quarter of Fiscal Year 2026 (April – June).
