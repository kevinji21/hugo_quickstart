*This summary is based on action minutes. Full summary minutes were not available for this meeting.*

## Summary
The Palo Alto City Council convened on October 19, 2026, for a regular meeting, addressing a range of community and administrative topics. Key discussions included a presentation from Caltrain, celebrating a sister city relationship, and approving several items on the consent calendar, notably funding for a community grant program, a partnership for child care, and an ordinance regarding oversized vehicle parking. The Council also made a decision on a tree removal appeal and authorized legal action in support of a neighboring city.

**Key Actions/Decisions/Discussion Items:**
*   Received a presentation from Caltrain on a "no external funding" scenario, taking no action.
*   Celebrated Palo Alto's relationship with Tsuchiura, Japan.
*   Approved $15,000 for the Know Your Neighbors Grant Program.
*   Approved a Letter of Intent for a public-private partnership to expand child care at Cubberley.
*   Adopted an ordinance prohibiting overnight parking of oversized vehicles in residential areas, with one dissenting vote.
*   Approved reducing a special setback along Hansen Way at 3300 El Camino Real.
*   Approved over $1.2 million for Zoom Government Unified Communications Solutions for five years.
*   Upheld a decision to allow the removal of four protected Coast Redwood trees at 751 Waverley Street.
*   Authorized the City Attorney's Office to file an amicus brief in support of the City of Menlo Park in a litigation case.

## Agenda Highlights

*   **Caltrain Presentation:** Caltrain shared information about a potential scenario where they would not receive external funding, but the Council did not take any action on this item.
*   **Celebrating Tsuchiura, Japan:** The Council recognized and celebrated Palo Alto's sister city relationship with Tsuchiura, Japan, and acknowledged Denis Shovkoplyas, the city's 2026 Tsuchiura Marathon Runner.
*   **Approval of Minutes:** Minutes from the September 28 and October 5, 2026, meetings were officially approved.
*   **Know Your Neighbors Grant Program:** The Council approved allocating $15,000 from its contingency fund to support the "Know Your Neighbors" Grant Program, which fosters community connections.
*   **Child Care Partnership at Cubberley:** A Letter of Intent was approved with Palo Alto Community Child Care to explore a long-term partnership aimed at expanding child care services at the Cubberley site.
*   **Overnight Oversized Vehicle Parking:** The Council adopted a new rule to prohibit the overnight parking of oversized vehicles in residential areas. They also supported a pilot program for parking permits on Embarcadero Road. Councilmember Lauing voted against this measure.
*   **Special Setback Map Amendment:** An ordinance was approved to reduce a specific building setback requirement along a section of Hansen Way at 3300 El Camino Real, changing it from 50 feet to 20 feet.
*   **Zoom Communications Solutions:** The Council approved two purchase orders totaling over $1.2 million to acquire Zoom Government Unified Communications Solutions for city use over the next five years.
*   **Tree Removal Appeal (751 Waverley Street):** The Council heard an appeal regarding the removal of four protected Coast Redwood trees at 751 Waverley Street. After review, they confirmed the Director's original decision to allow the trees to be removed.
*   **Commission Interviews:** The Council had planned to interview candidates for the Public Art Commission and the Stormwater Management Oversight Committee but took no action on this item.
*   **Legal Action in Support of Menlo Park:** Following a closed-door discussion, the Council unanimously decided to authorize the City Attorney's Office to file an amicus brief, which is a legal document offering information or arguments, in support of the City of Menlo Park in an ongoing lawsuit (Willow Project LLC v. City of Menlo Park et al.).
