## Summary
The Palo Alto City Council held a special meeting on September 28, 2026, addressing various city operations, community partnerships, and development matters. Key discussions included selecting candidates for city commissions, approving partnerships for new community spaces at Cubberley, and advancing several infrastructure and permitting system contracts.

Key actions and discussions included:
*   Reviewed applications and selected candidates for interviews for the Human Relations, Planning and Transportation, and Stormwater Management Oversight Commissions.
*   Approved Letters of Intent and Term Sheets for public-private partnerships to develop a multi-generational makerspace and a Recreation Wellness Center at the Cubberley site.
*   Approved several contracts for city services, including a new building permitting system, a waste characterization study, and utility engineering support.
*   Approved the final map and street naming for a condominium subdivision at 3200 Park/340 Portage.
*   Conducted a preliminary review for a commercial addition to a historic resource at 706 Cowper Street.

## Agenda Highlights

*   **Item 1: Caltrain Presentation:** This item was removed from the agenda and was not discussed by the Council.
*   **Item 2: Commission Candidate Selection:** The Council reviewed applications and selected candidates to interview for upcoming vacancies on the Human Relations Commission, Planning and Transportation Commission, and Stormwater Management Oversight Committee.
*   **Item 3: Closed Session – Potential Litigation:** The Council met privately with the City Attorney to discuss potential litigation, as allowed by state law. No public action was taken.
*   **Items 4-13 (Consent Calendar):** These items were approved together as part of the Consent Calendar, meaning they passed without individual discussion unless a Council Member requested otherwise.
    *   **Item 4: Meeting Minutes Approval:** The Council approved the minutes from its August 19, September 9, and September 14, 2026 meetings.
    *   **Item 5: Cubberley Makerspace Partnership:** The Council approved a Letter of Intent with Maker Nexus to collaborate on a long-term public-private partnership for a new multi-generational makerspace at the Cubberley site.
    *   **Item 6: State Law Enforcement Funds:** The Council approved accepting $211,000 in state funds from the Citizens Options for Public Safety (COPS) program and amended the budget to support municipal police services.
    *   **Item 7: Cubberley Recreation Wellness Center Partnership:** The Council approved a Term Sheet with Friends of the Recreation Wellness Center to move forward with a public-private partnership for a new recreation wellness center at the Cubberley site.
    *   **Item 8: Building Permitting System Contract:** The Council approved a five-year contract, not to exceed $3.48 million, with TruePoint Solutions (GovPath) for the licensing, maintenance, and support of the City’s building permitting system.
    *   **Item 9: Waste Characterization Study:** The Council approved a contract, not to exceed $270,233, with Cascadia Consulting Group, Inc. to conduct a study of the city’s municipal solid waste stream.
    *   **Item 10: Utility Engineering Systems Support:** The Council approved an amendment to a contract with CAD Masters, Inc., increasing the total amount to $2.37 million and extending support for utility engineering systems and applications through August 2029.
    *   **Item 11: Storm Drain Project Contract Extension:** The Council approved an amendment to a contract with Schaaf & Wheeler Consulting Civil Engineers, extending the term through June 30, 2027, for several storm drain upgrade projects.
    *   **Item 12: 706 Cowper Street Preliminary Review:** The Council conducted a preliminary review for a proposed amendment to the Comprehensive Plan Land Use Plan Map, which would allow a roughly 400 square foot commercial addition to a historic building located in a residential area.
    *   **Item 13: 3200 Park/340 Portage Final Map:** The Council approved the final map to subdivide a 3.92-acre parcel for condominium development and approved the naming of the new private streets.
