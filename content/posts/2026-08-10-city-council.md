## Summary

The Palo Alto City Council convened on August 10, 2026, for a regular meeting that primarily focused on a comprehensive Consent Calendar. During the session, the Council approved various administrative items, authorized significant contracts for city infrastructure, and advanced key initiatives related to environmental sustainability and financial oversight.

Key actions and decisions included:

*   Approving the City Auditor's Annual Risk Assessment and the audit plan for the upcoming fiscal year.
*   Establishing guidelines for a new grant program aimed at supporting public agency electric vehicle charging hubs.
*   Authorizing several contracts for essential city services, including upgrades to traffic signal networks, maintenance of electric substations, and legal services.
*   Extending and amending contracts for the Regional Water Quality Control Plant's facilities plan and the South Palo Alto Bike/Ped Connectivity Project.
*   Adopting the property tax levy for library bonds and accepting grant funding for intelligent transportation systems.

## Agenda Highlights

Here's a look at the key items discussed and decided by the City Council:

*   **Approval of Minutes:** The Council formally approved the minutes from their meetings held on June 8 and June 15, 2026.
*   **Extend City Auditor Task Orders:** The Council agreed to extend the deadlines for certain tasks assigned to the City Auditor for the 2026 fiscal year, with no changes to their approved budgets.
*   **City Auditor Annual Risk Assessment and FY 2027 Audit Plan:** The Council approved the City Auditor's yearly assessment of financial risks and the plan for audits to be conducted in the 2027 fiscal year.
*   **Public Agency Electric Vehicle Charging Hub Grant Program:** Guidelines were approved for a new grant program designed to help public agencies install electric vehicle charging stations.
*   **FY 2027 Property Tax Levy for Library Bonds:** The Council adopted the property tax rate for the 2027 fiscal year, specifically to cover payments for the library projects funded by bonds issued in 2008.
*   **VRF-ITS Cycle 5 Grant Program Funding:** The Council accepted grant funding from the Vehicle Registration Fee-Intelligent Transportation Systems program and adjusted the 2027 fiscal year budget to incorporate these funds.
*   **Natural Gas Purchase Contract:** A contract was approved with JPMorgan Chase Bank, N.A. for the purchase of a portion of the city's natural gas supply.
*   **Amend Municipal Code (Contracts and Claims):** The Council adopted an ordinance to increase the spending limits for soliciting goods and services, as well as the authority for approving contracts and settling claims, aiming to streamline city operations.
*   **Purchase Traffic Signal Network Switches:** A purchase order was approved for $714,639 over two years with JAM Services, Inc. to acquire new traffic signal network switches, with a corresponding budget adjustment.
*   **Repair Adobe Creek Substation Equipment:** A contract for $286,660 (up to $315,326 with potential change orders) was approved with High Point Power Systems for the repair and refurbishment of a specific piece of equipment at the Adobe Creek Substation.
*   **Electric Substations Painting Services:** A contract for $735,500 (up to $809,050 with potential change orders) was approved with Blackhawk Painting Inc. for painting services at electric substations over 18 months, along with a budget amendment.
*   **Legal Services Contract Amendment:** The Council authorized an amendment to a legal services contract with Atkinson Andelson Loya Ruud & Romo, increasing the amount by $100,000 to a new total of $285,000.
*   **Regional Water Quality Control Plant Facilities Plan Update:** An amendment was approved to increase the contract with Carollo Engineers, Inc. by $551,755 (for a total of $3,294,529) and extend the term by six months for work on the Regional Water Quality Control Plant's long-range facilities plan.
*   **South Palo Alto Bike/Ped Connectivity Project:** An amendment was approved to extend the contract term for Kittelson & Associates through September 2028 and update the scope of services for the South Palo Alto Bicycle and Pedestrian Railroad Crossing Study, with no increase to the total compensation.
