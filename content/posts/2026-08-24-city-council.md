## Summary

The Palo Alto City Council held a Special Meeting on Monday, August 24, 2026, addressing a range of city business. Key discussions and decisions included selecting candidates for a city board, reviewing police oversight, approving several administrative items, authorizing agreements for a significant housing redevelopment project, and launching new pilot programs to manage oversized vehicle parking in the city.

Key actions and discussions included:
*   Reviewed applications and selected candidates for interviews for the Historic Resources Board.
*   Held a semi-annual discussion with the Independent Police Auditor regarding police oversight.
*   Approved minutes from a previous meeting and a response to a Civil Grand Jury report on pothole damage.
*   Authorized a purchase for furniture for a new city building and an amendment to a contract for the University Avenue Streetscape project.
*   Gave final approval to an ordinance increasing thresholds for city contracts and claims settlements.
*   Authorized the City Manager to execute regulatory agreements for the Buena Vista Mobilehome Park redevelopment.
*   Approved the launch of two pilot programs for Oversized Vehicle (OSV) parking, including a permit system, fees, designated permit districts, and prohibitions on other streets.

## Agenda Highlights

**1. Historic Resources Board Candidate Selection:** The Council reviewed applications from residents interested in serving on the Historic Resources Board and selected individuals to move forward to the interview stage.

**2. Independent Police Auditor Discussion:** Council members held their semi-annual meeting with the Independent Police Auditor (IPA) to discuss police oversight, review recent activities, and address any questions or concerns regarding police department accountability.

**3. Approval of August 10, 2026 Meeting Minutes:** The Council formally approved the minutes from their meeting held on August 10, 2026.

**4. Response to Civil Grand Jury Report:** The Council approved the city's official response to a Civil Grand Jury report titled "Pothole Damage: Improving Claims Processes within Santa Clara County."

**5. Stanford University Traffic Control Agreement:** This item, which involved approving a five-year agreement with Stanford University for traffic control services during stadium events, was removed from the agenda.

**6. Furniture Purchase for New Lab Building:** The Council approved a purchase order of $441,056 for new furniture for the upcoming Laboratory and Environmental Services Building project.

**7. Contract Amendment for University Avenue Streetscape Project:** An amendment was approved for a contract with CSWST2, adding $170,450 and extending the term to December 31, 2028. This is for preliminary design work and community engagement for the University Avenue Streetscape improvement project.

**8. SECOND READING: Municipal Code Amendments (Contracts & Purchasing):** The Council gave final approval to an ordinance that updates the Palo Alto Municipal Code. These changes increase the financial limits for how the city solicits goods and services, approves contracts, and settles claims.

**9. Buena Vista Mobilehome Park Regulatory Agreements:** The City Council authorized the City Manager to sign new and updated regulatory agreements related to the redevelopment of the Buena Vista Mobilehome Park at 3980 El Camino Real.

**10. Oversized Vehicles (OSV) Parking Pilot Programs:** Following a recommendation from the OSV Ad Hoc Committee, the Council approved the launch of two pilot programs for oversized vehicle parking. This included adopting a new ordinance for an OSV parking permit program, establishing permit fees, creating specific permit districts on parts of Embarcadero Road and Coyote Hill Road, and prohibiting OSV parking on numerous other streets throughout Palo Alto. Related penalties and budget adjustments were also approved.
