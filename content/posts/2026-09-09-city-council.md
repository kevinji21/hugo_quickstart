## Summary

The Palo Alto City Council held a special meeting on September 9, 2026, addressing a variety of city matters. Key discussions included interviews for the Historic Resources Board, a proclamation for the 25th anniversary of September 11th, and performance reviews for top city officials. The Council also approved numerous agreements and policy updates via the consent calendar, and held a public hearing on rezoning a property to support housing goals.

Key actions and decisions included:

*   Interviewed candidates for the Historic Resources Board.
*   Issued a proclamation honoring the 25th anniversary of September 11, 2001.
*   Conducted performance evaluations for the City Manager, City Attorney, and City Clerk in a closed session.
