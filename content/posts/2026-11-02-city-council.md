## Summary

The Palo Alto City Council held a regular meeting on November 2, 2026, addressing a range of city business from housing policy to climate goals and routine operational approvals. Key discussions included making state housing laws permanent in local codes and reviewing the city's environmental progress.

Key actions and discussions included:

*   Adopting a new ordinance to permanently incorporate state housing laws, like SB 9 and SB 450, into Palo Alto's building and zoning rules.
*   Reviewing the city's 2025 Greenhouse Gas Inventory and progress towards climate action goals.
*   Approving the City Council and committee meeting calendar for 2027.
*   Referring the topic of electric system long-duration resiliency to city staff for further review.
*   Approving several routine contracts for city services and materials, and an audit report.

## Agenda Highlights

**Closed Session:** The Council met privately with the City Attorney to discuss potential litigation, as is standard practice for legal matters.

**Study Session: Review of 2025 Greenhouse Gas Inventory and Climate Action Progress:** The Council received and discussed a report detailing the city's greenhouse gas emissions for 2025 and reviewed the progress made on key performance indicators for the city's Climate Action Plan. This was a discussion item to inform the Council about environmental efforts.

**Consent Calendar:** The Council approved the following items together without separate discussion:

*   **Meeting Minutes:** Approved the minutes from the October 19, 2026, meeting.
*   **Electric System Resiliency:** Referred a recommendation from the Climate and Sustainability Committee to city staff. Staff will now look into ways to make Palo Alto's electric system more resilient to long power outages.
*   **Janitorial Cleaning Services Contract:** Approved an amendment to increase funding for the city's janitorial cleaning services contract with SWA Services Group, Inc.
*   **Asphalt Materials Contract:** Approved a three-year contract with Graniterock Company to provide hot mix asphalt materials for the Public Works and Utilities Departments, used for road maintenance and other projects.
*   **Junior Museum & Zoo Audit:** Approved the audit report for the Junior Museum & Zoo, as recommended by the Policy & Services Committee.
*   **Affordable Housing Project Compliance:** Approved a special arrangement for an affordable housing development at 3150 El Camino Real, allowing for a split term in its regulatory agreement to meet city affordable housing requirements.

**City Manager Comments:** The City Manager provided updates on various city activities and initiatives.

**Action Items:**

*   **Public Hearing: Permanent State Housing Law Ordinances:** Following a public hearing, the Council adopted a new ordinance. This ordinance makes permanent changes to Palo Alto's building, zoning, and subdivision rules to comply with state housing laws, including Senate Bill (SB) 9 and SB 450, replacing temporary rules that were in place.
*   **2027 City Council Meeting Calendar:** The Council adopted a resolution establishing the annual calendar for City Council and Council Standing Committee meetings, recesses, and events for the upcoming year 2027.
