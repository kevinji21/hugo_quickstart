## Summary
The Palo Alto City Council held a regular meeting on November 9, 2026, addressing a range of community issues from new housing proposals to infrastructure improvements and city finances. Key discussions included a preliminary review of a large residential development, progress on a major transit connection, and advancing a significant bike and traffic calming project. The Council also approved several financial contracts and finalized new city ordinances.

*   Recognized National Police Records and Support Personnel Week.
*   Discussed a proposal for 200 new residential rental units at 720 San Antonio Road.
*   Reviewed 60% design plans for the Quarry Road Transit Connection.
*   Approved minutes, a Q3 priorities update, and contracts for energy efficiency and financial audit services.
*   Gave final approval to ordinances regarding city code amendments, a special setback reduction on Hansen Way, and a temporary moratorium on detention facilities.
*   Authorized the Bryant Street Bike Boulevard and Lincoln Avenue Traffic Calming Project to proceed to final design.
*   Met in closed session for real property negotiations.

## Agenda Highlights

**1. Proclamation Recognizing National Police Records and Support Personnel Week:** The Council issued a proclamation to honor the essential work of the city's police records and support staff.

**2. 720 San Antonio Road - Residential Development Prescreening:** Council held a preliminary discussion on a developer's request to rezone a commercial property to allow for an eight-story building containing 200 residential rental units. This "prescreening" helps the developer understand the Council's initial thoughts before submitting a full application.

**3. Quarry Road Transit Connection - Design Plans Review:** Council reviewed the 60% complete design plans for the Quarry Road Transit Connection project. This project aims to improve safety and access for pedestrians and cyclists in the area, and Council provided feedback on the current designs.

**4. Approval of Minutes from October 26, 2026 Meeting:** The Council formally approved the minutes from a previous meeting.

**5. 2026 Council Priorities and Objectives Q3 Update:** Council received an update on how the city is progressing on its key goals and priorities for the third quarter of 2026.

**6. Approval of Contract with Veolia North America for Efficiency and Electrification Program Services:** The Council approved a contract worth up to $3,012,410 over five years with Veolia North America. This company will help commercial customers in Palo Alto improve their energy efficiency and switch to electric systems.

**7. Approval of Contract Amendment with Macias Gini & O’Connell LLP (MGO) for Financial Audit Services:** The Council approved an amendment to an existing contract with MGO, increasing their compensation by $477,261 (for a new total of $1,491,651) and extending their services for two more years to continue providing external financial audits for the city.

**8. SECOND READING: Ordinance Amending PAMC Sections 10.44.015 and 10.44.020:** The Council gave final approval to an ordinance that makes changes to specific sections of the Palo Alto Municipal Code. (This ordinance had its first reading on October 19, 2026, and passed 6-1).

**9. SECOND READING: Ordinance Amending Special Setback Map for Hansen Way:** The Council gave final approval to an ordinance that reduces a special building setback requirement along Hansen Way at El Camino Real to 20 feet. (This ordinance had its first reading on October 19, 2026, and passed 7-0).

**10. SECOND READING: Ordinance Establishing Temporary Moratorium on Detention and Custodial Facilities:** The Council gave final approval to an ordinance establishing a temporary ban on the creation of new detention and custodial facilities within the city. (This ordinance had its first reading on October 26, 2026, and passed 7-0).

**11. Authorization to Advance Bryant Street Bike Boulevard and Lincoln Avenue Traffic Calming Project to Final Design:** The Council authorized city staff to move forward with the final design phase for the Bryant Street Bike Boulevard and the Lincoln Avenue Traffic Calming Project, which aims to make these areas safer and more accessible for cyclists and pedestrians.

**AA1. CLOSED SESSION: Conference with Real Property Negotiators:** The Council met in a private session to discuss ongoing negotiations related to the potential purchase, exchange, or lease of properties located at 3525 & 3543 Park Boulevard.
