## Summary

The Palo Alto City Council held its regular meeting on September 14, 2026, addressing a variety of city matters including infrastructure projects, financial decisions, and appointments to city commissions. Key actions involved approving significant contracts for city improvements, finalizing new parking regulations, and reviewing plans for a major downtown parking facility.

Key actions and discussions included:
*   Approved a contract for a three-year sewer lateral replacement project across the city.
*   Finalized an ordinance establishing parking permit districts for oversized vehicles.
*   Approved an increase in the construction budget for the new Public Safety Building project.
*   Held a public hearing for a proposed six-story parking garage at 375 Hamilton Avenue.
*   Authorized the refinancing of water revenue bonds to potentially save on interest costs.
*   Selected candidates for interviews and made appointments to various city commissions.

## Agenda Highlights

**1. Northern California Power Agency General Manager Tony Zimmer Presentation**
The Council received an informational presentation from the General Manager of the Northern California Power Agency (NCPA).

**2. Review Applications and Select Candidates to Interview for Commission Vacancies**
The Council reviewed applications and selected candidates to interview for open positions on the Human Relations, Parks and Recreation, Planning and Transportation, Public Art Commissions, and the Stormwater Management Oversight Committee.

**3. Appointment of Candidates to the Historic Resources Board (HRB)**
The Council made final appointments to fill vacancies on the Historic Resources Board.

**4. Approval of Contract for Sewer Lateral Replacement Project**
The Council approved a contract for up to $2.7 million over three years with A3 Pipeline for a project to replace sewer lines at various locations throughout the city.

**5. Adopt Resolution Approving Electric Utility Physical Security Plan Update**
A resolution was adopted to approve an updated physical security plan for the City of Palo Alto Utilities Electric Utility.

**6. SECOND READING: Ordinance Adding Chapter 10.49 (OSV Parking Permit Districts)**
The Council gave final approval (second reading) to an ordinance that establishes parking permit districts for oversized vehicles and amends a related section of the city's municipal code. This ordinance had previously passed its first reading unanimously.

**AA1. Approval of Increase of Construction Contingency for New Public Safety Building**
The Council approved an increase of over $7.3 million to the construction budget for the new Public Safety Building project. This increase supports a settlement agreement with the builder, Swinerton Builders.

**7. PUBLIC HEARING / QUASI-JUDICIAL: 375 Hamilton Avenue Parking Garage**
A public hearing was held to review a request for major architectural approval to construct a new six-story parking garage with 265 spaces on the existing surface-level parking lot at 375 Hamilton Avenue.

**8. Refinancing of the 2009A Water Revenue Bonds, Series A**
The Council adopted a resolution authorizing the issuance and sale of new tax-exempt water revenue refunding bonds, up to $20 million, to refinance existing bonds from 2009. This action aims to potentially reduce future interest costs for the city.

**9. CLOSED SESSION: CONFERENCE WITH CITY ATTORNEY-EXISTING LITIGATION**
The Council met in a private session with the City Attorney to discuss existing legal matters.
