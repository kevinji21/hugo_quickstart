## Summary

The Palo Alto City Council held a special meeting on Tuesday, October 13, 2026, to address a single action item. The primary focus of the meeting was to discuss the potential release of documents related to an offer to purchase the Cubberley Community Center property.

**Key Discussion Item:**
*   Consideration of whether to make public documents concerning an offer to buy the Cubberley Community Center property.

## Agenda Highlights

**1. Potential release of documents related to offer to purchase Cubberley Community Center property.**
The City Council convened to discuss and consider the potential release of specific documents. These documents are connected to an offer that has been made to purchase the Cubberley Community Center property. The meeting's purpose was to deliberate on whether these materials should be made public.
